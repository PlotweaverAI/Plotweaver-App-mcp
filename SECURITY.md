# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately to **info@plotweaver.app** with "Security" in the subject. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and its impact. Do not open a public issue.

We acknowledge reports within two working days and keep you informed until the issue is resolved.

## Scope

- The MCP server at `https://mcp.plotweaver.app/mcp`, its sign-in pages and its upload and recording pages
- The developer API at `https://api.plotweaver.app`
- The dashboard at `https://app.plotweaver.app`
